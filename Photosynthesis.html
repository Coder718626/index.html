And (yes, i know you arent actually reading this because who the hell cares about forces?) we're gonna do... Types Of Forces!!

All of the types of forces are: Gravitational Force, Drag Force, Applied Force, Spring Force, Electric Force and Magnetic Force.

So this is how everything works! (yes, this took me a long time to write): Gravitational Force: The force of attraction between two objects due to their mass. It is always attractive and acts over an unlimited range. Drag Force: Also known as air resistance, it opposes the motion of an object moving through a fluid. It is a type of friction force. Applied Force: The force exerted by a person or another object to move an object. Spring Force: The force exerted by a spring when it is compressed or stretched. Electric Force: The force that acts between charged particles, such as protons and electrons. It can be attractive or repulsive. Magnetic Force: The force that acts between magnetic poles, which can be either attractive or repulsive. These forces play crucial roles in various physical phenomena and are fundamental to understanding the behavior of objects in the universe.
